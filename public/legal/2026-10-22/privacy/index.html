3. Camera, selected photos and device processing

Camera permission lets you capture a menu. The photo picker provides the image you select rather than general access to your photo library. You can manage camera permission in iOS Settings.

The current menu-scanning flow reads text on your device. The normal scan request sends candidate menu lines and language context to our server, not the menu photograph. The current flow does not send GPS coordinates or a destination-country value with that request. Photograph upload is a separate, optional improvement flow described below. This statement concerns the current menu scanner, not a promise that every device permission is a server transfer.

The scan can temporarily hold an image in app memory for processing. The optional improvement copy is re-encoded and server-sanitized to remove embedded metadata, including EXIF/GPS, before storage. This does not remove personal information visibly written or pictured in an image; avoid submitting it.

4. Catalog matching and AI

Our server first checks available catalog information. If an AI description is needed and you have granted the current AI-sharing permission, OpenAI receives the candidate menu-item text and any applicable country context to generate dish information. Our generation request does not include your account email, account identifier, full scan history or menu photograph. Do not include personal details in menu text.

OpenAI processes API requests under its applicable business terms and data controls. Its published default API policy does not use API content for model training unless the customer opts in, and allows abuse-monitoring retention generally up to 30 days, subject to exceptions. We do not promise zero retention or an account-specific regional-processing arrangement without verifying it. See https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/your-data.

Permission to share with a third-party AI service is separate from optional analytics and photo improvement. Before your first eligible scan, the app offers an AI-sharing choice identifying the recipient and information sent. You can change this choice in Profile → Settings → Privacy settings. Without permission, catalog matching remains available, while AI-dependent results are unavailable. Turning AI sharing off on an offline iPhone stops new AI-enabled submissions there; queued server work cannot learn the withdrawal until it reconnects. Requests already sent to OpenAI cannot be recalled. General catalog facts may be reused independently of your private scan records; your account history and optional personal photo copies are not published in the shared catalog.

5. Optional choices

Analytics: When offered and enabled, PostHog receives a limited set of app events under a random, account-specific analytics identifier only after permission to the current notice. It is pseudonymous, not anonymous: Visual Plate associates it with your account to manage consent and deletion. It is not your account or device identifier. Event payloads exclude menu photos, menu text and email. See the Analytics Notice for network metadata and retention details. In the current reviewed configuration the project key is empty, so no optional analytics events are sent.

Photo improvement: If you opt in, a separate sanitized menu-photo copy and associated recognition results/findings can be used by authorized reviewers to evaluate OCR and dish matching. These copies stay in restricted storage, are not public and are not sent to an external AI provider for model training. Copies reach their retention deadline after 30 days, with earlier removal after withdrawal, rejection or account deletion. Scheduled cleanup runs every 15 minutes and retries failures. The Photo Improvement Notice explains the process.

Change these choices in Profile → Settings → Privacy settings. Permission starts only after it is saved by the server. Turning a choice off stops new optional collection on that iPhone immediately. If offline, the app retains the withdrawal request and retries; other devices cannot learn an offline change until it reaches the server. A changed notice may require a fresh choice. Withdrawal does not make earlier lawful processing unlawful.
