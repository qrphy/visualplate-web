Photo Improvement Notice

Version: 2026-10-21 · Effective: 21 October 2026

Photo improvement is optional and off unless you choose to allow it. Scanning, your five free dishes and your subscription allowance work without it. Camera permission and accepting Terms do not enable it.

If you allow it, Visual Plate can upload a separate copy of menu photos you submit, linked to that scan's recognition results and review findings. Authorized reviewers can use them to investigate OCR errors, missing dishes, wrong matches and catalog-content errors and evaluate corrections. Reviewers may view the sanitized photo and linked scan results; access is restricted and recorded.

The server removes embedded image metadata, including EXIF and GPS, before storing the copy in private Supabase storage in Ireland. Visible faces, personal text and other private information are not automatically erased by metadata removal; avoid including them. The developer operates from Türkiye. The Privacy Policy explains recipients and international processing.

These copies are not public catalog photographs, are not sent to external AI providers by the improvement workflow and are not used to train those providers' models. This permission is for controlled review and evaluation, not unrestricted reuse or a transfer of ownership.

Copies and their linked evaluations reach their retention deadline 30 days after receipt. The scheduled cleanup runs every 15 minutes and removes expired copies on its next successful run. Withdrawal, reviewer rejection or account deletion starts earlier removal. Turning this choice off in Profile → Settings → Privacy settings stops new collection on that iPhone immediately. If the phone is offline, it retries the change; server-side collection and review are disabled once the server records withdrawal. An upload already in flight may need to finish its short upload window before its object is removed.

Removal of database records and physical storage objects happens in separate steps. Failed cleanup is retried; previously fetched browser or CDN copies and backup copies follow their respective cache or provider cycles. We do not claim every copy is erased at the moment the switch changes. Independently sourced public catalog facts corrected during review may remain, without retaining your photo or personal scan history for improvement.

To withdraw on another device, wait for the server-saved preference or turn the choice off there as well. You can also request deletion or ask about outstanding removal at visualplate800@gmail.com. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of earlier processing. Controller: Furkan Titiz; contact and postal address are in the Privacy Policy.
