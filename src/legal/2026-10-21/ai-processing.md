## Optional choice
When our catalog cannot supply a dish, Visual Plate can send that dish's menu text to OpenAI's API to identify and describe it. Your explicit choice is separate from the Terms, analytics and photo improvement. AI processing starts off. Allow records this notice version on your account; Not now continues with available catalog results. A failed AI-dependent item does not spend a delivered-dish allowance.

## Information shared
The request contains the dish/menu text extracted on your phone and, if supplied, a destination country. The current MVP sends no destination. We do not include your menu photo, account email, account ID or device ID in this provider request. Avoid menu text containing personal information. AI output can be inaccurate; it cannot confirm allergens or a restaurant's actual ingredients.

## Provider handling
OpenAI processes this text using its API service. API data is not used to train models by default unless the account opts in. Standard abuse-monitoring logs may contain request/response content and are generally retained for up to 30 days, with possible longer retention where legally required. Visual Plate does not claim Zero Data Retention or a European-only processing arrangement. Data may be processed outside your country. The Privacy Policy describes international processing and applicable rights.
Official information: https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/your-data

## Changing your choice
Use Profile > Privacy settings > Use AI for dish information. Turning it off prevents AI in new scans on this iPhone immediately. The server checks its current recorded permission immediately before queued requests. Offline withdrawal is retried when connected: already queued server work may continue until the withdrawal reaches the server. A request already sent to OpenAI cannot be recalled. Previously delivered Library results remain available under the Library/account policy.

## Permission records
We retain the notice/version, choice and time as an audit record. Account deletion removes the account and its scan data through the existing deletion process; previously sent provider data follows the provider's applicable retention controls.
